cBioChat: How Interaction Modality Shapes Genomic Data Exploration

cBioChat brings LLM-driven natural-language interaction to cBioPortal. With Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center, we run a controlled 2×2 study that separates how researchers express intent from how results are presented, measuring how each shapes exploration, verification, and trust.
